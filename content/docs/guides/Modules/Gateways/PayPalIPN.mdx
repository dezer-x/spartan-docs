# PayPal IPN Gateway Setup - Complete Guide

## Prerequisites

- PayPal Account
- Make sure u enabled PAYPAL INSTANT PAYMENT NOTIFICATIONS, and set the url to https://billing.example.com/payment/paypalipn/ipn
![IMG](/ipn.png)
## Step 1: Go to Spartan

1. **Configuration**
   - Go to Spartan and log in with a admin account
   - Go to admin overview then click on Modules link in sidebar.
   - Then go to payment gateways tab and click on PayPalIPN
   - Over there, add ur paypal email there. and select if its a sandbox or live account and your all done!
