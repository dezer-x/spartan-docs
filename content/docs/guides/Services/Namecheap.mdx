<Callout type="warn" title="Namecheap API requirements">
  To enable the Namecheap API, your account must meet one of the following requirements:
  Without meeting one of these requirements, the Namecheap API cannot be enabled.
</Callout>

- Have $50+ in Namecheap account credit
- Have at least 20 active domains in your Namecheap account
---

## Access Namecheap API Settings

1. Log in to your **Namecheap** account.
2. Navigate to **Profile → Tools**.
3. Under **Business & Dev Tools**, click **Manage**.

![Step 1](/services/namecheap/step-1.png)

---

## Enable the Namecheap API

1. Enable the **Namecheap API**.
2. Copy your **API token** and store it securely.
3. Whitelist the **IP address of your DezerX Spartan instance**.
4. Click **Done** to save your changes.

![Step 2](/services/namecheap/step-2.png)

---

## Configure DezerX Spartan

1. Open **DezerX Spartan**.
2. Go to **Admin → Modules → Services**.
3. Select the **Namecheap** service.
4. Fill in all required fields using your Namecheap API details.

![Step 3](/services/namecheap/step-3.png)

---

Once enabled, Namecheap functionality will be available through the main portal rather than the admin product system.
